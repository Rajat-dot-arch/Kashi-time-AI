Location: Manikarnika Ghat

History:
Manikarnika Ghat is one of the oldest and holiest cremation ghats in Varanasi. Its name is linked to the legend of Sati’s earring, or manikarnika, falling here, and to Vishnu digging the Manikarnika Kund. It has been used as a cremation ground for centuries and is mentioned in ancient traditions.

Significance:
It is considered the most auspicious place for Hindu cremation and is associated with moksha, or liberation. The nearby Tarakeshwar Shiva lingam is closely linked with the cremation ground. The ghat remains a powerful symbol of Varanasi’s relationship with death and salvation.

Changes Over Time:
- Ancient period: Established as a major cremation ghat.
- 1770: Renovated by Ahilyabai Holkar.
- Modern period: Continues as a working cremation ground with regulated facilities.

Key Events:
- 1770: Ahilyabai Holkar renovation.
- Present: Continuous cremation rituals.
