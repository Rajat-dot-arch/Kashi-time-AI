Location: Ramnagar Fort

History:
Ramnagar Fort is a sandstone fort on the eastern bank of the Ganga, opposite Varanasi. It was built in 1750 by Kashi Naresh Balwant Singh. It remains the residence of the Kashi Naresh and includes a museum.

Significance:
It is a royal and cultural landmark. The museum displays vintage cars, palanquins, weapons, costumes, and manuscripts. The fort hosts the annual Ramnagar Ramlila, a famous traditional performance of the Ramayana.

Changes Over Time:
- 1750: Built by Balwant Singh.
- 20th century: Museum established.
- Present: Royal residence and tourist site.

Key Events:
- 1750: Construction.
- Annual: Ramnagar Ramlila.
- 20th century: Museum opened.
