Location: Assi Ghat

History:
Assi Ghat is the southernmost major ghat of Varanasi, located at the confluence of the Assi River and the Ganga. It is mentioned in ancient Puranic texts. The present ghat was rebuilt by Ahilyabai Holkar in the 18th century and later renovated in the modern period.

Significance:
It is a center for morning rituals, yoga, and cultural events. The Subah-e-Banaras morning aarti and music program is held here. Tulsidas is traditionally believed to have lived nearby, giving the area literary and devotional importance.

Changes Over Time:
- 18th century: Rebuilt by Ahilyabai Holkar.
- 2014: Subah-e-Banaras program started.
- 2010s: Revamped with better steps and public spaces.

Key Events:
- 18th century: Ahilyabai Holkar reconstruction.
- 2014: Morning cultural program launched.
