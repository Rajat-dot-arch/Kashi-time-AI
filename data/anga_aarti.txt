Location: Ganga Aarti

History:
Ganga Aarti is a daily evening ritual honoring the Ganga River. The best-known aarti takes place at Dashashwamedh Ghat in Varanasi. The modern public form with large lamps, incense, and chants developed in the late 20th century.

Significance:
It expresses devotion to the Ganga and is a major cultural attraction. Priests perform synchronized rituals with fire, flowers, and bells. The aarti has become one of Varanasi’s most recognizable public ceremonies.

Changes Over Time:
- Late 20th century: Public aarti popularized.
- 2020: Pandemic restrictions and live streaming.
- Present: Daily at Dashashwamedh Ghat, also at other ghats.

Key Events:
- Late 20th century: Modern aarti format.
- 2020: Live-streamed during COVID-19.
