Location: Bharat Mata Mandir

History:
Bharat Mata Mandir is a temple dedicated to Bharat Mata, or Mother India. It was built by Babu Shiv Prasad Gupta and inaugurated by Mahatma Gandhi in 1936. The main shrine contains a marble relief map of undivided India.

Significance:
It represents India as a mother and was linked to the Indian independence movement. It is a unique temple because it does not have a traditional deity. Instead, the map of India is the object of reverence.

Changes Over Time:
- 1936: Inaugurated by Mahatma Gandhi.
- Post-independence: The marble map continues to show undivided India.
- Present: Heritage and nationalist site.

Key Events:
- 1936: Inauguration by Mahatma Gandhi.
