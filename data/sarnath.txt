Location: Sarnath

History:
Sarnath is a Buddhist site about 10 km from Varanasi. It is where Gautama Buddha delivered his first sermon after enlightenment, known as the Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta. Emperor Ashoka built stupas and monasteries here in the 3rd century BCE. The site declined after the 12th century and was rediscovered and excavated in the modern era.

Significance:
It is one of the four holiest Buddhist pilgrimage sites. The Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa, and Ashokan Lion Capital are major monuments. The Lion Capital is India’s national emblem.

Changes Over Time:
- c. 528 BCE: Buddha’s first sermon.
- c. 250 BCE: Ashoka builds monuments.
- 5th century CE: Dhamek Stupa constructed.
- 12th century: Decline.
- 1794/1835: Modern rediscovery and excavation.
- 1905: Sarnath Museum opened.

Key Events:
